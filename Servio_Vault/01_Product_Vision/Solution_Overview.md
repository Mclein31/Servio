# 💡 The Solution: "Foodpanda for Services & Skills"

**SERVIO** transforms the chaotic world of local home repairs and trade services into a frictionless, on-demand mobile commerce experience modeled after modern food and grocery delivery apps (e.g. Foodpanda, Grab, Uber Eats).

---

| **Restaurant Menu** | **Service Category & Service Catalog (SKU Menu)** |

| **Customer Review & Tip** | **Two-Sided Star Rating, Tagged Feedback, and Platform Guarantee** |

## 2. Core Pillars of the Solution

### A. The Standardized Service SKU Engine
Instead of posting a vague request and waiting hours for conflicting quotes:
- Services are broken down into **standardized catalog packages** with fixed base prices.
- *Example:* "Aircon General Cleaning" is not a negotiation. It has a pre-defined checklist: filter cleaning, blower wheel pressure wash, coil chemical spray, drain pipe flush, and operating test.
- If a customer is unsure of the defect, they book a fixed-fee **"Diagnostic & Inspection Visit"** (e.g., $15 fixed callout fee), which applies as a credit if repair work is subsequently authorized.

### B. Algorithmic Proximity Dispatch (The Radar)
- When a customer taps "Book Now", SERVIO broadcasts the order to all online, verified technicians within a 10km radius possessing the matching trade credentials.
- The nearest available technician receives an urgent audio chime and a 60-second acceptance card showing distance, job scope, and payout.
- Eliminates phone calls, schedule negotiation, and multi-day waiting periods.

### C. Live On-the-Way GPS Tracking
- Once a technician is en route, the customer sees the technician's real-time avatar moving along the route on a live map, complete with turn-by-turn ETA.
- Completely removes the anxiety of "Will they show up?"

### D. Frictionless In-App Add-on Quotation
- If on-site inspection reveals an unpredicted fault (e.g., punctured copper coil or burnt capacitor):
  1. The technician selects the standard part from an in-app catalog or inputs the verified part price with a photo of the broken component.
  2. The customer instantly receives an in-app prompt: *"Technician recommends replacing Capacitor (+$25). Tap to Approve."*
  3. No awkward verbal haggling or off-platform coercion.

### E. Escrow & The 4-Digit Completion OTP
- Money is safely locked in platform escrow during the entire job.
- When the technician finishes the service, they request the customer's **Completion OTP**.
- The customer only provides this code once they inspect the working appliance.
- Entering the OTP unlocks the escrow, credits the technician's wallet immediately, and triggers the 30-day platform service warranty.
