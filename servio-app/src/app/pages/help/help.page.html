How can we help?

Common questions

How do I book a service?

Open Directory, choose a helper and select a fixed-price service. Confirm your address and schedule at checkout, then authorize the simulated hold.

Browse helpers
How do I accept a Bulletin request?

Use your Servio Pro account, open a request and tap Accept request. You must be approved and offer that exact service. Wait for the customer to confirm the booking before starting work.

Open Bulletin
Why hasn't my payment been charged?

Payments, refunds and payouts are currently simulated. The app does not charge your card or GCash account.

Payment preferences
How do cancellations and completion work?

Open the booking in Tracking to view its status and available cancellation action. Share your completion PIN only after inspecting the finished work. Report unresolved work or payment issues below.

Open Tracking

Stay safe together

  • Keep job communication in the booking chat.
  • Never share sign-in codes, passwords or payment credentials.
  • Approve extra work through the booking before it begins.
  • Check the helper's identity before allowing access to your property.
  • If you are in immediate danger, contact local emergency services. This form is not an emergency channel.
